Zainab Hanjra is a Master’s student in Bioinformatics at KU Leuven, Belgium. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Biotechnology from Forman Christian College University (FCCU), Pakistan, where she graduated with First Class Honours.

During her undergraduate studies, she worked as an Undergraduate Research Assistant at the Kauser Abdulla Malik School of Life Sciences, FCCU, under the supervision of Dr. Aftab Bashir. She subsequently joined the Nuclear Institute for Agriculture and Biology (NIAB), Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission, as a Research Fellow under the supervision of Dr. Muhammad Rashid, Technical Liaison Officer for the NIAB–IAEA Collaborating Centre.

Her research experience across experimental biology and computational genomics has deepened her interest in bioinformatics and the application of computational approaches to biological research. She is particularly interested in regulatory genomics, multi-omics integration, and machine learning approaches for understanding complex biological systems.
