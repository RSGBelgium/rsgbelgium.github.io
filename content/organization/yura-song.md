Yura Song is an FNRS Research Fellow (Chargée de recherches) and bioinformatician at the Université libre de Bruxelles (ULB), working in the Laboratory of Stem Cells and Cancer led by Prof. Cédric Blanpain.

Her research focuses on understanding how mechanical cues and oncogenic mutations reshape epithelial cell identity, influence cell competition, and contribute to tumour initiation. By integrating single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, multi-omics technologies, and computational approaches, she investigates the molecular mechanisms and cellular interactions underlying tissue homeostasis, epithelial plasticity, and cancer development. She has contributed to studies published in leading scientific journals, including Nature and Nature Cancer.

Beyond her research, Yura is passionate about advancing open and reproducible computational biology, fostering interdisciplinary collaborations, and strengthening the bioinformatics community. She actively participates in international scientific conferences and community-building initiatives, including RSG Belgium.

With a background in medical translation and scientific communication, she is also interested in bridging the gap between computational research, biological discovery, and the wider scientific community. She believes in making complex scientific knowledge more accessible and creating opportunities for collaboration across disciplines, languages, and cultures.
